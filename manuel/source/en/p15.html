SettingValuesTip
Battery8 S to 20 SNumber of cells in series in the board's pack. Essential for an accurate percentage. Lite Foil: 14 S.
SpeedLOW · MED · HIGHDefault power mode at power-on. Beginner: LOW.
LimitOFF · 12 to 40 km/h (steps of 2)GPS speed limit. The receiver smoothly lowers the throttle ceiling when the speed goes over the limit and raises it again below: the trigger stays proportional, never a cut-out. Accuracy 1 to 2 km/h (the GPS lags by one second). Designed for a child, a beginner or a rental; “L25” on the screen, orange when it is acting. Without a GPS fix, no limiting.
Capacity-- · 10 to 100 AhCapacity of the board's battery. Enables the estimated range: the remote counts the Wh used since contact with the board, the average power (60 s) and the Wh per km, and shows “25min 9km” next to MAX while riding, the remaining Wh when stopped. The starting level comes from the resting voltage; board switched back on with another battery = counter reset to zero. An estimate, not a measurement: keep a margin.
Sensitivity0 · 1 · 2 · 30 = very progressive, 3 = linear.
Startup boost0 to 24 % (steps of 3)Minimum thrust on the first millimetre of trigger. 6 to 9 % for a clean take-off.
Unitskm/h · mphAlso changes the odometer and the timed run distances.
PingON · OFFShows the radio response time on the riding screen. Diagnostics only.
GyroON · OFF · --Tilt menus (section 16). ON starts the three-step learning, A cancels it. “--”: sensor not detected. Can also be set from the app once the learning is done.
Auto disarmOFF, 1 to 10 sAfter a fall, the motor locks itself once the delay without throttle has passed. 3 to 5 s recommended.
ThemeBlack/Spd · Black/BatBoard speed or battery in large type in the centre of the riding screen.
LanguageFrançais · English · Español · DeutschAll screens and messages.
Distanceread-onlyDistance travelled by the remote since it was made, added up from the GPS.
