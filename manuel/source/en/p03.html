02 Technical characteristics

Hardware

ProcessorESP32-S3 dual-core, 240 MHz, 16 MB flash memory (two firmware slots for over-the-air updates)
Screen1.69" IPS color 240 × 280, 65,000 colors, LED backlight; all text in a rounded font (Nunito, 8 built-in sizes)
TouchCapacitive (CST816), software detection of taps and swipes
TriggerHall-effect sensor, 16 averaged readings, adaptive filter (smooth at rest, responsive when moving)
Buttons2 waterproof buttons A and B, short / long / combined presses
Inertial unitQMI8658 6-axis (accelerometer + gyroscope): tilt menus, fall detection, smoothed heading
ClockPCF85063 real-time clock: time available from power-on, set by the app, corrected by GPS
Logbook3 MB of flash memory: every session recorded second by second, read back in the app
WaterproofingIP69K (immersion and high-pressure jets)
Weight245 g
Battery1-cell Li-ion 5,750 mAh, managed by a dedicated BMS, % gauge on screen
ChargingDedicated port at the bottom of the grip; “CHARGING” screen with % and voltage; automatic shutdown 2 min after full charge
StandbyDeep sleep: wake up with a long press on A (3 s) or by the charger

Links

Board2.4 GHz radio (ESP-NOW), 50 commands/s to the receiver, telemetry 10 times/s in return, maximum transmit power
PairingBy proximity (less than one meter), 5 s window when the board powers on, only one remote linked, address filtering
BluetoothBluetooth Low Energy: Lite Foil app (settings, gauges, update) and GoPro / Insta360 cameras
UpdateWireless from the app, remote on the charger; settings and pairing are kept
GPSIn the board (receiver): speed, time, satellites sent up to the remote
Tracker (option)5 Hz GPS + 4G modem in the board: live tracking on sportstracklive.com, emergency alert

ServicesSportsTrackLive live tracking, emergency alert to your loved ones, timer with history (time, V.MAX, distance), GoPro / Insta360 camera control (REC, mode, auto-REC)
Customizationkm/h or mph, 2 screen themes (large speed or large battery), 4 languages, number of cells in the pack (8 to 20 S), radio ping display, lifetime odometer
