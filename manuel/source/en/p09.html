09 SportsTrackLive: live tracking tracker option

With the tracker installed in the board, your session is published live on the internet, with no phone on the water: someone on the beach or 500 km away can see where the rider is, their speed and their track, while they ride. The session then stays in their SportsTrackLive account (track, speed, max).

How to use it

  • Once: in the Lite Foil app, Tracker tab, enter the email and password of the SportsTrackLive account. They are stored in the tracker, not in the phone.
  • Every session: menu, SportsTrackLive tile, flip the switch (tap or B). The tracker turns on its 4G, connects to the account and opens the session as soon as the first GPS point comes in: the switch turns green with the session number.
  • Stop: same switch, or automatically when the remote switches off (immediately) or stays silent for 10 minutes.
  • The badge in the top bar of the riding screen shows the status at all times (section 4).

The tracker in a nutshell

Inside the board and powered by it: its own 5 Hz GPS, a 4G modem with an international SIM card, a secure connection to the site. With no network, the points wait and go out as soon as 4G comes back. After a power cut, it resumes a session less than 10 minutes old. A board sold without a tracker keeps exactly the same remote: the tracker can be added later with three wires.
