Maintenance

  • Rinse with fresh water after every session at sea, dry before charging.
  • Charge with the supplied charger; don’t leave the battery flat for several weeks.
  • Don’t leave the screen in the sun, switched off, in a closed car.
  • Install the updates offered by the app: they bring fixes and features.

Warnings

  • Only ever arm the motor when you are ready, trigger at rest, board in the water and clear.
  • Disarm before climbing back onto the board, going near the propeller or handing the remote to someone.
  • Live tracking and the emergency alert depend on 4G coverage: they do not replace mandatory safety equipment.
  • Wear the wrist strap: a lost remote cuts the motor within 0.5 s, but the board drifts.
