Introduction

The Lite Foil remote controls the board by radio, shows you live everything the rider needs to know and runs the board's services: online GPS tracking, emergency alert, camera, timer. You hold it in one hand, trigger under your index finger, screen right in front of your eyes, and everything is done with two buttons or with a finger on the screen.

Top: screen (1), button A on the left, button B on the right
Side: trigger (2) under the index finger
Grip: charging port (3) at the bottom

Orientation: remote in hand, screen facing you, the blue pad under the screen carries two buttons. A is the left one (power symbol: a circle broken by a line), B is the right one (lightning symbol). The rest of the manual uses these two letters throughout.

1 · 1.69" touchscreen: color, 240 × 280, readable in full sunlight. Touch works only in the menus, never while riding or with the motor armed.
A · Left button (power): power on (3 s), arm / disarm the motor, confirm, go back, power off (2 s).
B · Right button (lightning): power mode, timer (3 presses), emergency (5 s), “next” and “change” in the menus.
A + B together (150 ms): opens and closes the menu from the riding screen.
2 · Trigger: contactless magnetic sensor (Hall effect), no wearing parts, read 50 times per second, guided calibration at every power-on.
3 · Charging port at the bottom of the grip: plugging in disarms and locks riding, and turns on Bluetooth for the app.
Inertial unit: 6-axis accelerometer and gyroscope for the tilt menus, falls, flights and heading (section 16).
Radio: dedicated 2.4 GHz link with the board’s receiver, protected pairing, motor cut-off within 0.5 s if the link drops.
