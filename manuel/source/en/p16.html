GPS speed limit

SetLimit tile: B scrolls OFF, 12, 14 … 40 km/h (shown in mph if the unit is mph), A confirms. In the app: “GPS speed limit”, from 8 to 40 km/h in 1 km/h steps.
How it worksThe receiver compares the GPS speed with the limit 50 times a second. From 1 km/h below the limit, it lowers a throttle ceiling, all the faster the further you are over; below it, it raises it again slowly. The trigger stays proportional under the ceiling: you keep control, there is never a cut-out, and the ceiling never drops below 20 % so the board always has enough to keep going.
On screen“L24” at the top right of the dial: grey = limit set, orange = the receiver is limiting. In the app, remote status: “limit 24 km/h (active)”.
Accuracy1 to 2 km/h above the limit: the GPS lags by about one second and braking is deliberately gentle so as not to jolt the rider.
Without GPSNo fix (at the start, overcast sky): no limiting possible, the ceiling stays at 100 % and “L24” stays grey.
Who it’s forChild: 12 to 15 km/h. Beginner: 18 to 20. Lending or rental: 25. For yourself: OFF.
SafetyThe limit replaces neither LOW mode nor the safety features: releasing the trigger always cuts the motor, and auto disarm stays active.
RequirementsUpdated receiver (October 2026). With an older receiver the limit is simply ignored. Older remotes remain compatible.

Estimated range

SetCapacity tile: B scrolls --, 10, 15 … 60, 70, 80, 100 Ah; the capacity is written on the battery or its datasheet. In the app: “Board battery capacity”. Leaving “--” turns the feature off.
CalculationPack energy = capacity (Ah) × cells × 3.6 V. On first contact with the board, trigger released, the resting voltage gives the starting level. Then the remote adds up the controller’s watts as Wh used, works out the average power over the last 60 seconds and the Wh per km while flying.
On screenBottom line of the dial, next to MAX: “25min 9km” while riding (minutes at the average pace, km at the consumption rate), “410Wh” when stopped or as long as the average power stays below 100 W. In the app: “Estimated range”.
ResetBoard off for more than 60 s (battery change) or remote switched back on: counter back to zero, new starting level from the resting voltage.
AccuracyGood when you set off with a full battery. Battery half empty at the start: the initial level comes from the voltage curve, less accurate for the first few minutes. Wind, chop and weight change the Wh per km: it is an estimate, keep a 20 % margin to get back.
TipSet the capacity once and for all. When you get back, compare the % gauge with the range shown at the start; if they don’t tell the same story, correct the capacity.
