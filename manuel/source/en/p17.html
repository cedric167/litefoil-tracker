16 Gyroscope: tilt menus, falls, flights, heading

The remote has a built-in 6-axis inertial unit (accelerometer + gyroscope, read 50 times a second, no magnetic compass). It is used for four things: navigating the menus by tilting the remote, counting falls, measuring flights, and turning the return compass between two GPS positions.

Turning it on: learning in three steps

Menu, Gyro tile, B to switch to ON. The remote learns how you hold it: there is no “official” position.

1 · Hold it normally
without moving. As soon as your hand is steady, the green bar fills in 1 s.
2 · Tilt to the RIGHT
and hold the position: a clear tilt (≈ 20°), held for 0.6 s. Beep.
3 · Tilt TOWARDS YOU
and hold: the same thing towards you. Beep, “ON”, back to the menu.

A cancels at any time (short beep, the tile stays OFF). With no gesture for 30 s, it gives up. Redo it if you switch hands. “--” on the tile: sensor not detected.

A gesture, how it works

A gesture = tilt decisively, hold, then come back to the starting position. The action fires on the way back, with a beep. The threshold is personal: 60 % of the tilt shown during learning (at least 0.25 g, at most 0.55 g), axis by axis; tilting 25° while learning gives a threshold of about 15°. The acceleration is smoothed, so a jolt or a step won’t trigger a gesture. The tilt must be held between 0.12 s (a tremor doesn’t count) and 3 s (beyond that, the remote assumes your hand has changed position and starts again from there). You need 0.35 s between two gestures. After an update from a version older than 1.14.0, redo the learning to get the personal threshold. The rest position slowly follows your hand: holding the remote higher or lower triggers nothing, only decisive movements count. The rest position is picked up again at each screen change.

Never active on the riding screen, with the motor armed, on the charger, on the Emergency page or during a calibration. The Gyro setting itself can’t be switched by gesture.

Falls

A fall is counted when the remote takes a knock (over 2.5 g) or drops in free fall (over 0.12 s) while the board was riding (at least 8 km/h in the previous 3 s) and then stops (under 4 km/h in the following 4 s). Short beep at each fall, at least 8 s between two falls. Counted per session (end-of-timer screen, app) and per timer (Timed runs page). A wave taken while riding without stopping doesn’t count.

Flights

A flight is a continuous period at 8 km/h or more. It ends with a fall, or after 5 s below 4 km/h (deliberate stop). The Timed runs page shows the longest flight and the average time between two falls (timer time divided by the number of falls plus one).

Smoothed heading for the return

The heading comes from the board’s GPS (twice a second, above 3 km/h). Between two positions and when stopped, the gyroscope turns the compass ring along with the remote, after learning its drift during 2 s of stillness. This gyro heading is accepted for 120 s after the last GPS position; after that the page shows “frozen” until the board picks up speed again.

Tips

Wide, decisive gestures, remote held normally. If gestures are missed or fire on their own: redo the learning, calmly, in your real riding position. When walking or on a boat, use the buttons instead.
