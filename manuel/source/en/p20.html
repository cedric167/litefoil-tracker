19 The receiver: standard and tracker versions

The receiver is the electronic board housed in the board's compartment. It receives the remote's commands by radio, passes them to the motor controller (VESC) and sends back to the remote everything it displays: battery, power, temperature, GPS speed. It comes in two versions. The remote, the app and every control in this manual are the same for both.

Standard version
Radio control, safety features, board GPS, speed limit, return to start, motor telemetry.
Tracker version
Everything the standard version does, plus a GPS / 4G module: live tracking on the internet, emergency alert, rides kept in the SportsTrackLive account.

19 The receiver: features in detail

Standard version

Radio link

Dedicated 2.4 GHz radio at maximum transmit power. The receiver gets the trigger position 50 times per second and sends telemetry back 10 times per second. It only listens to its own remote's address: a nearby eFoil can neither listen in nor take control.

Fail-safe

With no command for 0.5 s, the receiver sets the motor to neutral; on its side the remote disarms. Silence is what cuts: remote lost, switched off or out of range, the motor stops.

Pairing

Accepted only during the 5 s after the board is powered on, with the remote less than a metre away. A single linked remote, stored and kept through updates; any other request is refused while it is connected (section 08).

Motor control and telemetry

The receiver passes the throttle to the motor controller (VESC) and reads from it, live, the pack voltage, the power and the controller temperature: these are the VOLT, WATT and TEMP at the bottom of the screen, replaced by NO LINK if the link drops.

Board GPS

Speed shown large and max speed of the ride, number of satellites, time that corrects the remote's clock, position and heading sent twice per second for the return compass.

GPS speed limit

The receiver compares the GPS speed with the limit set on the remote 50 times per second and smoothly lowers the throttle ceiling, never below 20 %, with no cut-off. Without a GPS fix, no limiting (section 15).

Starting point

Stored the first time you arm during the ride with a GPS fix, or reset by holding B on the Return page. Forgotten at power-off (section 10).

Speed limit and return to start need a receiver updated in October 2026. With an older receiver, the limit is ignored and the Return page shows "heading: update receiver".

Tracker version tracker option

The module

Inside the board, powered by it: its own 5 Hz GPS, 4G modem with an international SIM card, secure connection to the website, Bluetooth under the name LITEFOIL-TRACKER for the app.

Live tracking

Started from the remote's SportsTrackLive tile or from the app. The tracker turns on its 4G, logs into the account and opens the session at the first GPS point. Stopped by the same switch, when the remote is switched off, or after 10 minutes without it.

No network, nothing lost

Points wait and are sent as soon as 4G comes back. After a power cut, the tracker resumes the session if it is less than 10 minutes old.

Emergency alert

Hold B for 5 s on the riding screen, then B for 3 s on the EMERGENCY page: the GPS position goes to the account's emergency contacts, live tracking is forced on and kept on while the alert lasts (section 11).

SportsTrackLive account

E-mail and password entered once in the app, Tracker tab, checked and then written into the tracker, not the phone. "Erase account" removes them.

Status visible everywhere

Dot in the top bar (green: live, orange: waiting for GPS / 4G, grey: stopped, red: account refused, yellow: no account, !: alert). Tracker page: session number, points sent, network, satellites.

Advanced settings (app)

Interval between two points (1 to 60 s), delay between two uploads (5 to 3,600 s), SIM card APN, server address. A refused value keeps the previous one.

Update

Tracker tab, Update card, board switched on: 3 to 6 minutes, automatic restart. Tracker from before October 2026: the first time by USB cable on the receiver, then through the app.

Upgrading from standard to tracker. The tracker can be added to the board later with three wires. Nothing to change on the remote: pairing, settings, timed runs and logbook stay, and the SportsTrackLive tile goes from "NO TRACKER" to "TRACKING STOPPED". All that remains is to enter the account in the app.
Live tracking and the emergency alert depend on 4G coverage: they do not replace mandatory safety equipment.
