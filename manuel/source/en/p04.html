03 Getting started

Power on

Hold A for 3 seconds. A shorter press won't wake the remote, so it can't switch on by accident in a bag. The sequence takes about 5 seconds: a big “Hello” in the chosen language, then the Lite Foil logo, then the guided trigger calibration, then the red “DISARMED” screen. The remote always starts disarmed.

Guided calibration, every time you power on. The remote checks the trigger's rest position over 1.5 s of stable reading, without asking you anything. If the rest position has moved by more than 2% since last time (sensor, magnet, temperature), it also asks you to pull fully. It never blocks you: A skips the step and keeps the old value; no action for 6 s does the same. The motor can't spin during the sequence. Too short a travel is rejected (“TRAVEL TOO SHORT, magnet too far?”).

Power off

On the riding screen, motor disarmed, hold A: after 0.8 s the screen shows “Shutting down… keep A pressed”, and 1.2 s later the remote shows a big “See you soon”, one word per line, and goes to sleep. Letting go earlier cancels. Power off is refused while the motor is armed: you don't cut the control of a spinning motor. Before going to sleep, it asks the tracker to close the current session and freezes any running timer.

Auto sleep

After 20 minutes with no action at all (button, trigger, touch), and only if the motor is disarmed, the remote switches itself off. Never while on the charger, never while the app is connected.

Charging

Plug in the charger: the “CHARGING” screen appears with the percentage and the voltage, and a blinking green dot. The motor is disarmed the moment you plug in, and riding is blocked. Bluetooth switches on by itself for the app (settings, update). Once it has been full for 2 minutes, the remote switches off; the charger can stay plugged in. Plugging in a remote that is off wakes it straight onto this screen.
