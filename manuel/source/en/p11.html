11 Emergency (SOS) and timer

Emergency alert tracker option

From the riding screen, hold B for 5 seconds: a red bar grows along the bottom of the screen, then the EMERGENCY page opens. (You can also reach it from the “Emergency” tile in the menu.) On this page, holding B for 3 seconds fills the red bar and sends the alert: the tracker sends its GPS position to the emergency contacts of the SportsTrackLive account. The menu tile flashes and the red “!” badge shows in the top bar for as long as the alert is active. Live tracking is forced on and kept running, even if the remote goes silent.

To end it: on the EMERGENCY page, hold B for 3 s again. A leaves the page without triggering anything. Letting go of B before the bar is full cancels.

Timer

While riding, three quick presses on B: a “START” banner for 1 s, then the elapsed time replaces the clock in the top bar (green, flashing colon). Three quick presses again: stop, the time is shown big for 3 s together with the top speed and the distance covered during the run, then it stays in grey for 30 s before the clock comes back. A single press keeps its usual job of changing the mode; two presses do nothing.

The timer keeps running if you disarm, is frozen when you switch off and shown again in grey the next time you switch on. The last 10 timed runs are stored in the “Timed runs” tile: date and time, time, V.MAX and distance in the unit you have set, number of falls (a jolt on the remote while the board was moving and then stops) and longest flight (continuous stretch above 8 km/h), the most recent one in green. One run per screen, big: swipe left / right, B or tilt to go from one to the next, A or the chevron to go back.
