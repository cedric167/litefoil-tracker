14 Bluetooth, logbook and updates

Making the remote visible

Menu, “Bluetooth” tile, flip the switch: the remote advertises itself as LITEFOIL-REMOTE for 5 minutes, then turns itself off if nobody connects. On the charger, it switches on by itself; while riding with the motor armed, it is always off. The page shows the name, the version, the battery and the app's signal.

Logbook

The remote records every ride in its memory, one line per second as long as the board is linked: time, GPS position, speed, throttle, pack voltage, remote battery, mode and armed state. 70 KB per hour, 3 MB kept (the oldest rides are erased automatically), one file per power-on. Transfer to the app over Bluetooth, ten seconds per hour of riding; nothing goes out to the internet.

Firmware update, step by step

  1. Remote on the charger (“CHARGING” screen, Bluetooth already on). Phone with internet.
  2. App, Remote tab, “Connect”, pick LITEFOIL-REMOTE.
  3. Update card: the installed version and the available version are shown, with what's new. Tap “Update to x.y.z”.
  4. The remote's Bluetooth page shows a blue bar and “UPDATING, do not switch off”. Buttons and touch are ignored. About 2 minutes.
  5. Automatic restart: “Hello”, logo, trigger calibration. The new version can be read on the Bluetooth page and in the app.

After the update
KeptPairing with the board, trigger calibration, all settings, gyro learning, timed runs, odometer, logbook.
To redoNothing. If a version changes the format of the timed runs, the old history is simply reset; this is stated in what's new.
Interrupted during the transferThe old firmware stays in place and the remote restarts on it: simply start the update again.
“No update”The remote is already up to date, or the phone has no internet when the app checks.
TrackerTracker tab, Update card, board on, 3 to 6 min, automatic restart. First time (tracker from before October 2026): by USB cable on the receiver, then through the app.

If the connection doesn't work

SymptomCauseWhat to do
LITEFOIL-REMOTE missing from the listRemote's Bluetooth off (5 min elapsed, or motor armed)Flip the Bluetooth switch again, or put the remote on the charger
The “Connect” button offers nothingBrowser without Bluetooth (Safari, Firefox) or phone's Bluetooth offUse the app from Google Play or the App Store, or Chrome on Android; turn on the phone's Bluetooth
Connection lost after a few secondsToo far away, or the app went into the backgroundMove closer, keep the app in the foreground during the transfer
The update stops halfwayBluetooth dropped during the transferNothing is broken: the old firmware restarts, start the update again
“Bluetooth” page: “--” or errorBluetooth radio busy with the camera, or faultyFlip the switch once (retry), otherwise switch the remote off and on again
