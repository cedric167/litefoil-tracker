13 The Lite Foil app (iOS and Android)

One single app for the board, the tracker and the remote. It talks to the remote over Bluetooth and, through it, to the board. Everything you can set on the remote can also be set from your phone, on a bigger screen and with both hands free, and that's where you'll also find the timed runs, the logbook and the updates.

Install

PhoneHow
AndroidGoogle Play: search for Lite Foil Tracker. Or in Chrome, open cedric167.github.io/litefoil-tracker then “Add to Home screen”: same app, same icon.
iPhone / iPadApp Store: search for Lite Foil Tracker. Same tabs and same features as on Android.
App updateAutomatic: every time you open it with internet, the new version loads by itself. Nothing to reinstall.
AccountNo Lite Foil account. Only live tracking needs a SportsTrackLive account (section 09).
This manualAlways up to date in the app: Remote tab, User manual card, “Open” or “Download” (PDF).

Connect to the remote

  1. Remote on: menu, Bluetooth tile, switch to ON, the screen shows “VISIBLE”. On the charger, it happens automatically.
  2. In the app, Remote tab, Connect button, pick LITEFOIL-REMOTE in the phone's list.
  3. The remote shows “APP CONNECTED” and receives the date and time from the phone. The trigger gauge and the mode move live in the app.

Range: a few metres. Bluetooth turns itself off after 5 minutes without a connection, and always while riding: the app is used on land, never on the water.

Settings from the phone

Settings card: all the menu settings (battery, speed, GPS speed limit, battery capacity, sensitivity, startup boost, units, auto disarm, theme, language, gyro, camera) in drop-down lists, then Save to the remote. The remote applies them right away and confirms with a beep. Gyro is only offered once the learning has been done on the remote (section 16). Ring the remote helps you find it in a bag.

The six tabs

TabContents
HomeLive summary: top speed, battery, power, GPS, network, tracking, and the status of the four devices (tracker, battery, VESC, remote). Community (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram), help and manual.
TrackerSportsTrackLive account (login saved in the tracker), live status (GPS, 4G, session), start / stop tracking, advanced settings, tracker firmware update (section 14), tracker log.
BatteryBoard pack: voltage, current, percentage, temperature, voltage of each cell, BMS protections.
VESCVESC controller: voltage, motor and battery currents, power, temperature, RPM, faults.
RemoteStatus (version, battery, link, estimated range, falls and flights of the session), settings, live trigger gauge and mode, timed runs, logbook, firmware update, user manual.
Lite Foillitefoil.fr website: boards, accessories, user guide, contact, account, YouTube videos, access to SportsTrackLive.

Timed runs in the app

Timed runs card: the remote's last ten timed runs, dated, with time, top speed, distance, falls and longest flight. “Clear history” empties the list on the remote.

Logbook in the app

Logbook card: “List rides”, then “Open” a ride. Six tiles (duration, distance, top and average speed, motor time armed, start and end voltage), track on the map, three curves (speed, throttle, battery), Export GPX (Strava, Google Earth), “Delete this ride” or “Clear all”. Logbook details in section 14.
