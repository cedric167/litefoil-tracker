12 Camera: GoPro and Insta360

The remote controls an action camera over Bluetooth: start and stop recording without letting go of the handle, or automate everything with motor arming or the timer.

Brand selection
GoPro HERO 9+, Insta360 X / ONE
RS / Ace, Insta360 GO 3
Camera page (GoPro)
6 tiles, banner in the brand’s
colours

First connection

  1. Put the camera in Bluetooth pairing mode (GoPro: Preferences › Connections › Connect Device › Remote; Insta360: Bluetooth turned on in the app).
  2. Menu, “Camera” tile, choose the brand (tap the card, or B then A).
  3. On the camera page, “BLUETOOTH” tile: the remote searches and connects. “connected” appears in green in the banner. The connection is remembered: next time, the camera reconnects by itself as soon as it is switched on.

The six tiles

RECORDSTART / STOP. While recording, the tile turns red with the elapsed time, and “● REC” shows on the riding screen
MODEVIDEO · PHOTO · TLAPSE (time-lapse)
BLUETOOTHGO = connect the camera · OK = connected
AUTO-RECOFF · ARM: recording starts when you arm the motor · TIMER: it follows the timer
SAFE STOPRecording stops automatically 10 s, 30 s or 2 min after disarming, or OFF
BACKBack to the menu

Navigation: tap a tile or B = next, A = change / run, hold A or swipe to the right = back. Cameras tested so far: GoPro HERO (Open GoPro protocol); Insta360 X and GO drivers included, validation in progress.
