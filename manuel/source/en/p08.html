08 Pairing with the board

Do this once, when you first set things up or after changing the remote or the receiver. Pairing is stored on both sides and survives updates.

Procedure

  1. Board off. Turn on the remote, open the menu (A + B), “Pairing” tile, open it with A or by tapping the tile.
  2. The page shows “Bring the remote close to the RX”, “Searching…” and a 60 s countdown. The remote is already broadcasting its request.
  3. Power up the board, with the remote less than one metre from the receiver. The receiver only listens during its first 5 seconds.
  4. “RX nearby −48 dBm” appears in green: the remote picks the receiver with the strongest signal, confirms it by name, and the receiver answers.
  5. Green PAIRING OK badge for 1.5 s, then automatic return to the menu. Leave the menu (A + B): the red screen shows the link (signal, satellites, board battery).
NO RX after 60 s: the board was switched on too early or too late (5 s window), or too far away. Switch the board off, restart “Pairing”, then switch the board back on. A or the “‹” chevron cancel at any time.
Only one remote at a time. While the paired remote is connected, the receiver refuses any other request. To change remotes, switch the board back on with the old one turned off.
