05 Motor safety

ProtectionHow it works
Disarmed startEvery time you power on and every time the remote wakes from sleep, the motor is locked. It takes a deliberate press on A, with the trigger at rest (less than 6% of travel).
Red screenWhile the motor is locked, the screen is red with a big padlock: no possible doubt about the state, even out of the corner of your eye.
Link lossRemote: disarms if there is no telemetry for 0.5 s. Receiver: motor to neutral if there is no command for 0.5 s. Fail-safe: it's silence that cuts the motor.
Auto disarmAdjustable from 1 to 10 s (or OFF): with no throttle during that time, the motor locks itself. Ideal after a fall.
Disarm refused under throttlePressing A while the trigger is above 15% is ignored with a beep: release it first.
Power off refused when armedYou can't switch the remote off while the motor is armed.
Dead zoneA clean zero with hysteresis (7% to start, 4% to return): the motor doesn't twitch at rest.
Protected pairingThe receiver only accepts pairing during the 5 s after it is powered up, with the remote less than a metre away, and refuses any new remote while its own is connected. After that it only listens to its remote's address: a nearby eFoil can neither pick up nor send commands.
ChargingPlugging in the charger disarms immediately and blocks riding.
TouchThe touchscreen never responds on the riding screen, while charging, or with the motor armed.

06 Trigger, modes and riding settings

Power modes

ModeMax powerLetter on screen
LOW60%L green
MED80%M yellow
HIGH100%H red

Switch with a press on B while riding, or set the default mode in the “Speed” menu. The mode is remembered.

Sensitivity (4 curves)

Level 0: nice and gentle (quadratic response, ideal for learning). Level 3: linear, direct response. Levels 1 and 2 are in between.

Startup boost (minimum thrust)

From 0 to 24% in steps of 3: the thrust applied as soon as you leave zero, so you take off cleanly without having to hunt for the trigger's starting point.

Learning and calibration

While riding, any reading beyond a known end stop becomes the new end stop, and is saved. The guided calibration every time you power on (section 3) protects against drift. The “Calibrate throttle” menu opens a full manual calibration in two guided steps (step 1 / 2 trigger released, step 2 / 2 trigger fully pulled, value in millivolts shown live, A confirms each step), for a first setup or after changing a magnet. B or the chevron cancels at any time. Green dot “OK!” then back to the menu; red dot “TRAVEL TOO SHORT” if the measured travel is under 150 mV (magnet too far?), and the old calibration is then kept.

Board battery

The percentage shown is calculated from the pack voltage and the number of cells set in the “Battery” menu (8 to 20 S), with a realistic Li-ion discharge curve. Visual alert below 44 V.
